# 🩸 Blood Relay

> **When every second matters, finding the right blood donor shouldn't depend on forwarding WhatsApp messages.**

Blood Relay is an emergency blood-donor matching experience designed to help people find nearby blood donors quickly, clearly, and with minimal effort.

## 🚨 The Problem

In an emergency, families often struggle to find a suitable blood donor. They may have to:

- Call multiple people
- Forward messages across WhatsApp groups
- Wait for someone to respond
- Explain the blood group and urgency repeatedly
- Track whether a donor has accepted or declined

At the same time, donors often receive unclear requests and don't know whether the request is still active.

## 💡 Our Solution

**Blood Relay** connects people who urgently need blood with nearby matching donors through a simple and accessible experience.

The goal is to make the entire process:

**Request → Match → Contact → Confirm → Track → Close**

as simple as possible.

## 🎯 Core Features

### 🩸 Emergency Blood Request

Users can create a blood request quickly by selecting predefined information instead of typing everything manually.

### 📍 Nearby Donor Matching

The system helps identify nearby donors who match the required blood group.

### 🤝 Donor Confirmation

Donors receive a clear **"Someone needs you"** experience where they can respond to a request.

### 📊 Request Tracking

The requester can see the current status of their blood request and know what is happening.

### ✅ Request Closure

Once the required blood is arranged, the requester can close the request so other donors don't continue responding.

## ⚡ 3-Tap Rule

The most important UX principle of Blood Relay:

> **Sending an emergency blood request should take 3 taps or fewer.**

The interface uses:

- Large buttons
- Preset options
- Minimal typing
- One clear action per screen
- Simple language

The experience is designed to remain usable even when the user is stressed or has difficulty using precise touch interactions.

## 🩸 Supported Blood Groups

Blood Relay supports the 8 commonly used ABO/Rh blood-group categories:

- A+
- A−
- B+
- B−
- AB+
- AB−
- O+
- O−

## ⚠️ Edge Cases

Blood Relay also handles situations where the normal flow doesn't work.

### 1. No Donor Nearby

If no matching donor is available nearby, the user receives a clear explanation instead of being left waiting.

### 2. Donor Declined

If a donor declines the request, the system allows the requester to continue looking for another suitable donor.

### 3. Request Expired

When a request is no longer active, it is clearly marked as expired so donors don't respond to an outdated emergency.

## 👥 User Flows

### Requester Flow

```text
Emergency
   ↓
Select Blood Group
   ↓
Select Required Information
   ↓
Send Request
   ↓
Matching Donors
   ↓
Contact / Confirm
   ↓
Track Request
   ↓
Close Request
```

### Donor Flow

```text
Someone Needs You
        ↓
View Request
        ↓
Check Blood Group & Distance
        ↓
Accept / Decline
        ↓
Contact Requester
```

## ♿ Accessibility First

Blood Relay is designed around the idea that emergency interfaces should work for people under stress.

Our design focuses on:

- Large touch targets
- High readability
- Minimal typing
- Clear actions
- Simple navigation
- Status communicated with words and icons
- Reduced cognitive load

## 🏆 Design Goal

Blood Relay isn't just another blood-donation app.

Our goal is to design an experience where a person in an emergency can understand **what to do next without thinking about how the app works.**

> **Less searching. Less calling. Less waiting. More certainty.**
